# Airline Boarding Procedures 2026: Research Brief for Simulator

Scope: 15 carriers as requested (Spirit included, though it ceased operations on May 2, 2026 [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/02/business/spirit-to-halt-all-flights), [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/05/02/spirit-airlines-shuts-down/)). Structure covers each carrier's ordered groups, boarding style, narrowbody seatmap, and any published time, followed by cross-airline research and a simulator-input mapping table. Every claim cites a URL. Gaps are flagged.

## Cross-airline: measured or published boarding times and academic work

- **Steffen 2008 optimal method**: alternating rows, window-first outside-in; theoretical ~3.5 min on a 12-row mock ([arxiv 0802.0733](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.0733)). Steffen and Hotchkiss 2011 experimental test on a 12-row mock 757 with 72 passengers: WILMA 1.7x faster than block, Steffen 2x faster than block ([arxiv 1108.5211](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.5211)).
- **MythBusters ep 222 (2014)**: 173-seat mock with 5% "problem" passengers. Back-to-front 24:29, Steffen fastest ([mythresults.com](https://mythresults.com/airplane-boarding)).
- **Van den Briel and Villalobos 2005**: America West "Reverse Pyramid" (simultaneously back-to-front and outside-in) cut boarding ~2 minutes / 20% on full flights ([Interfaces 35(3):191-201](https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/orinte/v35y2005i3p191-201.html), [NPR 2006](https://www.npr.org/2006/06/21/5500783/plane-truth-seating-passengers-is-a-pain)).
- **Jaehn and Neumann 2015 survey**: EJOR 244:339-359 review of methods; notes travelling-in-group share up to ~50% of cabin ([sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0377221714009904)).
- **Schultz 2017/2018 stochastic model**: field measurements at A320/737 turnarounds, calibrated agent model; business pax 73% solo, tourists 19% solo ([Aerospace 5(1) 27](https://doi.org/10.3390/aerospace5010027), [Aerospace 5(1) 8](https://doi.org/10.3390/aerospace5010008)).
- **Airline field claims**: Spirit ~20 min A320 ([Forbes 2013](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tedreed/2013/05/21/spirit-airlines-we-board-an-a320-in-20-minutes/)); KLM 22 min then 17 min after redesign, 737-800 ([Forbes 2013](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tedreed/2013/11/16/klm-we-can-board-a-boeing-737-800-in-17-minutes/)); United projects 2 min saved with WILMA ([CNBC Select](https://www.cnbc.com/select/united-boarding-process-prioritize-window-seats/)).
- Gap: no public per-airline boarding-time series from DOT or Cirium; both publish only on-time performance ([Cirium OTP](https://www.cirium.com/resources/on-time-performance/)).

## Cross-airline: sampling fractions (label as estimates)

- **Elite share on a typical US domestic flight**: ~10% observed elite (estimate, from upgrade-list observations, [FlyerTalk aggregation](https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/milesbuzz/861981-percentage-elite-frequent-fliers.html)). Top-tier under 1% of loyalty base ([Wonder aggregation](https://askwonder.com/research/air-travelers-united-states-frequent-flyer-status-those-top-tier-status-tst8q4aqx)). Gap: no publicly disclosed per-airline number.
- **Frequent-flyer program participation**: 40-65% of US flyers carry any FF miles ([Wonder aggregation](https://askwonder.com/research/air-travelers-united-states-frequent-flyer-status-those-top-tier-status-tst8q4aqx)).
- **First/business upgrades vs paid**: no primary published number; historical trade-press estimates of 40-60% of domestic-US F seats going to upgrades/comps are widely cited but not sourced to a primary release. Label all such shares as unverified estimates.
- **Basic economy share**: US DOT publishes fares by bucket but not "Basic Economy" as a labeled class ([BTS airfares](https://www.bts.gov/air-fares)). Trade-press estimates range 15-30% of domestic ticket volume. Gap.
- **Pre-boarders**: no aggregate public figure; DOT requires airlines to pre-board any self-identified disability ([DOT preboarding notice](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/Preboarding%20Notice%20Final.pdf)). Practitioner estimates 3-8% of pax pre-board on typical US domestic flights (estimate, not sourced).
- **Group sizes**: Schultz reports 73% business solo, 19% tourist solo; Jaehn/Neumann cite up to 50% of cabin travels in groups on normal commercial flights. Use these as calibration priors.
